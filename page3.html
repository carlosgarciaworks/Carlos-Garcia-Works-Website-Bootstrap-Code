Love and Hate Tryptic

October 19, 2021

This project requires the creation of three photoshop projects that can be connected visually to form a tryptic, and be based on the concept of love and hate. For this project, I used black to connect all three of the artworks in a cohesive manner. For the left and right end of the artwork, I chose to represent the good and the bad of myself. The bad depicts me having a printing machine catching fire on my head, while the results shown on paper are incomprehensible to visually clear. The good side shows the joyfulness of living and the bliss of remembering the good memories you have. The middle represents the unknown in life, but persistence in seeking what lies ahead. For this project, I was inspired by works made by Don Hertzfeld and Keith Haring. The good side has images replaced due to the utilization of images not of my own.
