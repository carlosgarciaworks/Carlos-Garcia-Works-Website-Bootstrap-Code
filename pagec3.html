Without love, I am nothing.

April 29, 2024

The film is about my own implementation of bible verses into my own life and serves as an introspection on my regrets and pursuits in life. The Canon T7 was used to record in full high definition, and later edited in post to apply greyscale values and film grain. The footage of stars was taken in several frames in photo mode, compiled together in a sequence, removed dead pixels, and positioned the pictures in place to form a video still of the night sky. Special Thanks to my uncle Miguel Montiel for using his property to record this short film.
